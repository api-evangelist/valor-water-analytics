# Valor Water Analytics

> Valor Water Analytics was a San Francisco-based water analytics company that built
> financial data and dashboard tools for water utilities, including leak and loss
> detection through its hidden revenue locator. A Y Combinator, Imagine H2O, and
> 500 Global portfolio company, it was acquired by Xylem Inc. (completed
> February 2, 2018) and folded into Xylem's digital solutions portfolio, now
> represented by the Xylem Vue platform. The valorwater.com domain redirects to
> Xylem Vue, and no independent Valor Water API, developer portal, or
> documentation remains published.

## Status

- Acquired by Xylem Inc.; acquisition completed 2018-02-02
